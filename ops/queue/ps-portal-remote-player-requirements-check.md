PlayStation Portal looks like a handheld, which leads many buyers to expect a console you can play anywhere. Sony's pages describe something narrower. This checklist looks at one exact listing, the Midnight Black PlayStation 5 Portal Remote Player, and asks what has to be in place before it does anything.

It uses Sony's product page, its support page for cloud streaming and PlayStation.Blog, plus the Amazon product page. We have not used this device, streamed a game or measured latency. It quotes no price, discount, stock level or customer rating, and it makes no comparison with other handhelds.

## Does PlayStation Portal work without a PS5 console?
For Remote Play, no. Sony's product page says a PS5 console and an account for PlayStation are required. The console must be connected to broadband internet, powered on fully or in Rest Mode, and paired with the Portal.

There is a second route. Sony says that with a PlayStation Plus Premium membership you can stream select PS5 games from the cloud even when your console is switched off or in use by another account. The PlayStation.Blog describes a home screen with Remote Play, Cloud Streaming and Search tabs, and says a PlayStation Plus membership is not required for Remote Play. Our [PS5 disc or digital guide](/guides/ps5-disc-vs-digital-checklist/) covers the console side of this choice.

## What Wi-Fi do you need?
Sony's product page says the Portal requires broadband Wi-Fi with at least 5 Mbps for use, and recommends at least 15 Mbps for a better experience. It adds that the quality and connectivity of play can vary with your network. Home Wi-Fi is not required, and Sony says it can also be used wherever you have broadband internet Wi-Fi at that speed.

For cloud streaming Sony gives separate minimums for upload and download speeds: 5 Mbps to establish a session, 7 Mbps to stream at 720p and 13 Mbps to stream at 1080p. Read those as official figures, not promises of smooth play, because we have not tested any connection.

## What does cloud streaming add, and who can use it?
Sony's support page lists three requirements: a PlayStation Portal Remote Player, an account for PlayStation, and an active PlayStation Plus Premium membership. It says cloud streaming on the Portal is not available for child accounts and that availability depends on country or region, with the United States on the list.

Sony also notes that streaming is limited to PS5 games and that PS4 and PS3 games are not supported there. Some system features, such as party voice chat and game invites for select games, are not available while streaming. That subscription is a recurring cost on top of the device, so count it before you decide the Portal replaces a console.

## Which games are left out?
Sony's product page says games that require a VR headset, PlayStation VR or PlayStation VR2, or extra peripherals other than a DUALSHOCK 4, DualSense or DualSense Edge wireless controller, are not compatible. It also says games that must be streamed on PS5 with a PlayStation Plus Premium membership are not compatible with Remote Play, and that haptic feedback and adaptive trigger features work when the game supports them.

If your favorite games lean on a headset peripheral or a special accessory, check this list first. Our [controller compatibility guide](/guides/controller-compatibility-checklist/) covers how to read platform lines on pads.

## Who should skip this device?
Skip it if you have no PS5 and do not want a PlayStation Plus Premium subscription, because the Portal needs one of the two. Skip it if your Wi-Fi is slower than Sony's 5 Mbps floor, or if most of your games need a VR headset or special peripherals.

Also skip it if you want to play games on the Portal itself, since Sony describes it as a way to play PS5 games from your console or the cloud. For other console add-ons, see our [console storage aisle](/category/storage/).

## What should you write down before ordering?
Write down the exact product name, the Midnight Black color, the PlayStation 5 platform line, and Sony's requirements: a PS5 console for Remote Play, 5 Mbps Wi-Fi at least, and PlayStation Plus Premium for cloud streaming.

If every line fits your setup, the listing is a reasonable candidate. If any one is missing, read that page fresh before ordering.
