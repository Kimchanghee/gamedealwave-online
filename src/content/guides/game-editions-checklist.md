A single game can appear in half a dozen listings: standard, deluxe, gold, ultimate, collector's, and for Nintendo Switch 2, a game-key card version. The box art is often identical. This checklist is about reading the listing so that what arrives matches what you expected.

## Which platform is the listing really for?

Start with the platform line, because cross-generation releases make mistakes easy. A PS4 disc plays on a PS5 with a disc drive, but a PS5 disc does not play on a PS4. A Nintendo Switch game card generally runs on Switch 2, but a Switch 2 game does not run on the original Switch. Look for the platform text in the listing title and in the "platform" or "edition" selector; photos are not reliable.

## What format will actually arrive?

There are four common formats:

- **Full game on a disc or game card.** The data is on the media, though a day-one update may still download.
- **Code in a box.** The package contains a download code instead of a disc. Packaging often says "code in box" or "download code" in small print.
- **Nintendo Switch 2 game-key card.** Nintendo describes these as physical cards that **do not contain the full game data**. The card is a key: you insert it, download the game over the internet into system memory or a microSD Express card, and keep the card inserted to play.
- **Digital code delivered by email.** Common for "digital" listings; the code is tied to a platform and often a region.

If you buy games to lend, resell or play offline, the format matters as much as the edition. Game-key cards and codes need an internet connection and free storage at least the first time.

## What do deluxe and collector's editions add?

Higher editions normally add **extras around the game**, not a different game. Typical additions are cosmetic items, digital artbooks and soundtracks, early access windows, or a season pass for future downloadable content. Before paying more, list the extras the edition promises and decide whether you would buy them separately.

Collector's editions add physical items such as statues, steelbooks or art prints. Some collector's editions ship the game as a code, and a few ship **without the game at all**, expecting you to own it already. The contents list on the listing or publisher page is the only reliable answer.

## How do PS4-to-PS5 upgrades work?

Many PS4 games offer an upgrade to the PS5 version, free or for a fee depending on the publisher. Sony's support page explains that upgrading an eligible **PS4 disc** requires a PS5 with a disc drive and that the disc must stay inserted to play the upgraded version. PS4 games claimed through PlayStation Plus are not eligible unless stated. If you plan to upgrade, confirm the specific game offers it before choosing the cheaper PS4 edition.

## What about codes and regions?

Digital codes can be tied to a country or region. Xbox support notes that some codes are meant for a specific country or region that might not match your account's settings. If a code is sold as "global" or from another country, check that your account region is supported before buying. See the [gift card region checklist](/guides/gift-card-region-checklist/) for the same rule applied to wallet funds.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Buying a game-key card expecting to play without a download.
- Paying for a collector's edition that does not include the game you assumed was inside.
- Buying the PS4 version for a Digital Edition PS5 and planning a disc upgrade.
