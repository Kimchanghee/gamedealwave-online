Two controllers can share most of a name and still target different devices. That is the main buying problem with the Ultimate 2C family. The blue Ultimate 2C Bluetooth is the Switch-oriented model; the Ultimate 2C Wireless is described separately for Windows and Android. Start with the full model name, not a familiar shell or a listing thumbnail.

This checklist uses 8BitDo's Bluetooth product page, its Bluetooth FAQ and the separate Wireless specification page. We have not used either controller or measured performance. The exact Amazon option observed for this article was Color: Blue and Style: Ultimate 2C, with the heading identifying the Bluetooth version for Switch and Switch 2.

## Is Ultimate 2C Bluetooth the same as Ultimate 2C Wireless?

No: the Bluetooth and Wireless models have separate platform and connection specifications. The Bluetooth product page lists Switch and Switch 2, with Bluetooth and wired USB connectivity. The Wireless page instead lists Windows and Android and assigns different connection methods to those platforms. A shared brand and similar shape do not merge those lists.

That distinction changes the first check on the buying page. Look for the complete phrase Ultimate 2C Bluetooth Controller and the intended Switch platform. Then check the selected style and color. A seller's family page may contain several related items, so the currently selected option matters more than a comparison image farther down the page.

The Wireless model's PC polling-rate advertising is not a specification for this Bluetooth model. We do not transfer its numeric performance claims, battery figures or adapter contents here. Our [controller compatibility checklist](/guides/controller-compatibility-checklist/) covers the general distinction between a connection method and support for the device you actually want to use.

## What must a Switch 2 owner check about firmware?

A Switch 2 owner must check the controller firmware rather than assume that every boxed unit is already updated. 8BitDo's current Bluetooth product page explicitly tells Switch 2 users to update through its support site. It also lists platform system requirements, which are a different requirement from the controller's own firmware.

This does not mean that an update has been installed on the specific unit offered by a seller. The listing observation identifies the product and selected option; it does not inspect software inside a sealed box. Before choosing this model for Switch 2, be comfortable following the manufacturer's current update process and checking the result on the intended console.

The wake feature also needs careful wording. The product page labels shake-to-wake for Switch 1. We therefore do not promise the same wake behavior on Switch 2. Connecting to a platform and waking that platform from sleep are different capabilities, even when ordinary controller input is supported.

## How does wired USB setup differ from Bluetooth pairing?

Wired setup has a console setting to check as well as a physical cable. In its Bluetooth-controller FAQ, 8BitDo says to enable Pro Controller Wired Communication under System Settings, Controllers and Sensors on Switch, then connect the controller with a USB cable. Simply seeing a USB connector does not confirm that the console setting is correct.

For wireless use, follow the instructions for this exact Bluetooth model instead of the Wireless model's receiver instructions. The manufacturer's included-items list for the Bluetooth model names the controller, a USB cable and an instruction manual. We do not describe a USB wireless receiver as included in this package.

Plan which connection you will normally use before ordering. A living-room player may want Bluetooth, while a docked arrangement can make wired setup relevant. Keep those plans separate from charging and storage. Our [Switch microSD selection guide](/guides/switch-microsd-card-checklist/) addresses the independent storage decision, not controller compatibility or pairing.

## What do the extra bumpers and motion control change?

The extra R4 and L4 bumpers provide additional remappable controls, and the Bluetooth model includes six-axis motion control. 8BitDo describes fast button mapping without software. Those are the features to compare if your reason for replacing a controller is its control arrangement rather than its color.

Do not turn that feature list into a promise of better results in a game. Whether an extra bumper is useful depends on the action you intend to assign and the way you hold the controller. Before buying, name that action and consider whether reaching another bumper is actually the change you want.

Hall effect joysticks are another listed feature, confirmed in the manufacturer's FAQ. They identify the joystick technology; this article does not turn that description into a guarantee against every future fault. We have not measured accuracy, service life or input delay. The official Bluetooth page's trigger descriptions are inconsistent, so trigger technology is omitted rather than inferred from the Wireless model.

## Who should choose a different controller instead?

Choose a different model if your main platform is a Windows PC or Android device and you specifically need the connection setup advertised for the Ultimate 2C Wireless. That is a separate product comparison. This Bluetooth article does not certify an unlisted platform just because a device might detect a connection.

Also compare alternatives if you require a feature that the manufacturer's Bluetooth specifications do not explicitly confirm for your console. A familiar button layout is not proof that all first-party functions are present. For Switch 2 in particular, confirm the current firmware instructions and distinguish basic connection support from the Switch 1 wake-on-shake feature.

The practical shortlist is simple: exact Bluetooth model, Blue color, correct platform, acceptable update process and a useful reason for the extra bumpers. Check those five points against the selected offer before ordering. This keeps the choice grounded in the setup you need, rather than borrowed specifications from another Ultimate controller.
