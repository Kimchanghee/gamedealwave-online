Xbox Series X|S consoles fill up fast, and the Seagate card is the plug-in route Microsoft documents for extra space. This checklist looks at one exact listing, the 1 TB Seagate Storage Expansion Card for Xbox Series X|S, and asks which consoles and games it fits before you order.

It uses Xbox support, the Xbox accessory page, Seagate's support page and data sheet, and the Amazon product page. We have not used this card, installed a game on it or measured load times. It quotes no price, discount, stock level or customer rating, and it makes no comparison with other storage.

## Which consoles can use the card?
Xbox support's answer is short: the card is compatible with Xbox Series X|S only. It is not compatible with Xbox One or Windows devices. The Xbox accessory page lists the same two consoles, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, and gives a release date of November 2020.

That makes it a poor match for anyone who still plays on an Xbox One or hopes to use the card as a PC drive. If you own only an Xbox One, a USB drive is the documented path, and our [Xbox storage guide](/guides/xbox-series-storage-checklist/) walks through the console choices.

## Which games can it hold and play?
Xbox support says the card performs identically to the console's internal SSD. You can play games that are Optimized for Xbox Series X|S from it, and it also plays Xbox One, Xbox 360 and original Xbox games. Support adds that thousands of those older titles show improvements, such as faster load times, when installed to the card.

This is the main difference from external USB drives. Xbox support says you can store games on any compatible storage device (USB 3.1, with a 128 GB minimum for games on external storage), but games optimized for Xbox Series X|S must be installed on the internal SSD or the Storage Expansion Card to be played. If you only want to park games you are not playing, a cheaper drive may do, which our [Xbox card versus USB comparison](/picks/c50-1tb-xbox-expansion-vs-usb-play-check/) covers for another model.

## How does setup work, and what about updates?
Xbox support says to remove the protective cover and plug the card firmly into the storage expansion slot on the back of the console. It also says to take your very first Xbox update before connecting a card.

The first time you connect it, the console asks whether you will use the card mainly with that console or with several consoles. Choosing the single console lets the card check for updates automatically. Choosing several consoles disables automatic updates for games and apps, though you can still update manually from My games and apps. You can change that choice later under Storage devices in the same menu.

## Can you move it between consoles or swap cards?
Yes, with conditions. Xbox support says you can take the card out and use it in another Xbox Series X|S console, and that sign-in or a game disc is required to play. The same answer applies if you own several cards and want to swap them.

Seagate's FAQ describes the same idea as plug-and-play, and says to remove the card and take your games with you. None of this applies to an Xbox One or a PC, which the card does not support.

The 1 TB capacity was the selected option, and a 2 TB option was also listed. It was filed under Electronics, then Computers and Accessories, then Data Storage, then External Solid State Drives.

Match those against the details on the listing you open. If the page shows a different capacity or a drive that does not name Xbox Series X|S, read it fresh.

## Who should skip this card?
Skip it if your console is an Xbox One or if you want storage for a Windows PC, because Xbox says the card is for Series X|S only.

Skip it if you only need space for older games, since a USB 3.1 drive is allowed for those. And skip it if you would rather not buy a slot-only accessory, because the card has no job outside the expansion slot. For other consoles, see our [console storage aisle](/category/storage/).

## What should you write down before ordering?
Write down the exact product name, the 1 TB capacity, the model STJR1000400, and Xbox's statements: Series X|S only, same performance as the internal SSD, and sign-in or a disc to play on another console.

If every line fits your setup, the listing is a reasonable candidate. If any one is missing, read that page fresh before ordering.
