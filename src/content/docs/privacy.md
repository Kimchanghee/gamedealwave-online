This policy explains what data GameDealWave (gamedealwave.online) collects and how third-party services on the site use cookies.

## What do we collect directly?

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, comments or forms. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply and handle your request.

## Google Analytics 4

When enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and links are used (for example, a click on an outbound product link). Analytics may set cookies and processes your IP address in shortened form. Google's practices are described at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Google AdSense and third-party cookies

Pages may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and advertising storage are **denied by default** (Google Consent Mode v2) until you make a choice in the consent message shown by Google's certified consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in that message.

## Product links

Product links go to Amazon.com. Once you leave this site, Amazon's own privacy notice applies. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Your rights and contact

You can ask what we hold about you (normally only an email you sent) and ask us to delete it: [contact@gamedealwave.online](mailto:contact@gamedealwave.online). See also the [terms of use](/terms/).
