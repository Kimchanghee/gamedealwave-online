GameDealWave links to products on Amazon.com. This page explains what those links are and how they may pay the site.

## Do product links earn the site money?

Today, **no**. Product links currently go to plain Amazon.com product pages without an affiliate tracking tag, so the site earns nothing from them.

The site intends to join the **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. When that happens, product links will carry an Associates tag, and every page with Amazon links will show this statement near the links and in the footer:

> As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

The notice in the site footer always reflects the current state of the links.

## Does a commission change what we recommend?

No. Products are chosen with the process described in our [methodology](/methodology/), before any link is created. A commission never raises the price you pay.

## Why are there no prices or star ratings?

Amazon prices, availability and ratings change constantly, and Amazon only allows them to be shown when they come directly from its official product data feed. We do not use that feed, so we show none of them. Check the current price on Amazon before buying.

## What about ads?

Some pages may show advertising served by Google AdSense. Ads are labelled and are separate from product links. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for how ad cookies work and how to opt out.
