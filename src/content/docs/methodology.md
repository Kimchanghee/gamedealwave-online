This page describes the process behind every checklist and product pick on GameDealWave. It is the same process for every article; nothing on the site is exempt.

## How is a topic chosen?

We start from a buyer question, not a product. Questions come from our public backlog: things like "will this SSD fit a PS5?" or "can I use a US gift card on a UK account?". Before writing, we search the question, read three to five of the pages that currently rank for it, and note what they answer well and what they leave out. We do not copy them; we write the missing parts.

## Where do the facts come from?

Specifications, compatibility rules, region restrictions and warranty terms come from **official pages**: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo and Steam support documentation, and manufacturers' product pages. Every article lists those sources at the bottom and shows the date the facts were checked. When an official source does not say something, we say it is not stated rather than guessing.

## How is a product pick chosen?

A product pick covers one product in one of our five aisles (consoles, games, gift cards, storage, controllers). Before a pick is published:

1. The product link is opened and confirmed to land on the **exact model and option** described (capacity, colour, edition, region).
2. Specifications are copied only from that product page or the maker's official page.
3. The article explains who the product fits, who should skip it, and what to confirm at checkout.
4. The post must pass an automated publish gate that checks the disclosure, link consistency, sources, length, and banned phrases such as invented first-hand testing.

## What do we never do?

- Print Amazon prices, stock, or star ratings.
- Claim to have tested a product we have not tested.
- Publish fake reviews, ratings or testimonials.
- Use product photos we do not have the right to use.
