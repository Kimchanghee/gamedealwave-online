GameDealWave is an independent, English-language website that writes **buying checklists for video game purchases**: consoles, game editions, digital codes, gift cards and wallet funds, console storage, and controllers. It is not a store, it does not sell anything, and it is not affiliated with Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Valve or Amazon.

## Why does this site exist?

Game listings hide the details that decide whether a purchase works for you: which console revision is in the box, whether a "physical" game is only a download code, which region a gift card is locked to, whether an SSD fits the PS5 slot with its heatsink. Those details are published by the platform holders, but they are scattered across support pages. We collect them into short checklists you can run through before you pay.

## What will you find here?

- **Buying checklists** (the [guides](/guides/)): evergreen explainers with a checkout slip of things to confirm, each linked to the official sources it relies on and dated with the day the facts were last checked.
- **Product picks** (the [picks](/picks/)): single-product posts that point to one exact listing, once the link has been opened and matched to the exact model and option.

## Editorial standards

1. **Facts come from official sources.** Specifications, compatibility rules and region policies are taken from the platform holder's or manufacturer's own pages, linked at the bottom of every article.
2. **No invented experience.** We do not claim to have tested, owned or used a product unless that is literally true, and we do not publish ratings, scores or reviews we did not produce.
3. **No prices.** Prices and stock change constantly. We never print Amazon prices, availability or star ratings; you see them on the retailer page.
4. **Dates are visible.** Every page shows when it was last updated and when its facts were last checked.
5. **Corrections are welcome.** If something is wrong, email [contact@gamedealwave.online](mailto:contact@gamedealwave.online) and we fix it and change the updated date.
