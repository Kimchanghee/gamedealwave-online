GameDealWave publishes buying information for game-related products. Please read these limits.

## Facts are dated

Every page shows when its facts were checked. Console revisions, region policies, gift card rules and storage requirements can change after that date. If a platform's official page says something different from ours, the official page wins; please [tell us](/contact/).

## No prices, no stock, no ratings

We never show prices, availability or star ratings. Anything about value is general guidance, not a statement about a current price.

## Third-party listings

Retail listings are created by sellers, not by us. A listing can change its contents, seller or region after we checked it. Always confirm the exact item on the retailer page before you pay.

## Not professional advice

Nothing here is financial, legal or technical support advice. For warranty and account problems, contact the manufacturer or platform. Read our [methodology](/methodology/) and [terms of use](/terms/).
